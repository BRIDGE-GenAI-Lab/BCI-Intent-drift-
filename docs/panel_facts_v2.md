# Physician-panel results (v2) - source of truth

Generated by `idrift.adjudicate.analyze_panel` from the three completed rater
sheets; digest at `output/human_rating_v2/panel_results.json`.

## Panel
- 1,295 blinded items: 980 message-critical + 315 stratified (all 5 CER levels x
  all 7 models; 259/CER, 185/model).
- Two physicians rated every item independently, blinded to model, CER, corpus,
  and the automated label. A third physician adjudicated disagreements.

## Inter-rater agreement (physician 1 vs physician 2), three-class label
- Raw agreement 88.0%; Cohen kappa 0.79 (substantial).
- Per-class kappa: faithful 0.93, drift 0.72, degraded 0.64.
- 1,139 items agreed on the three-class label; 156 required adjudication.
- Finer 4-level scale (message-critical drift split out): raw agreement 81.5%.
  Criticality among both-drift items agreed POORLY (kappa 0.13; rater 1 flagged
  33% of drifts critical, rater 2 3%). Reported as a limitation; no
  physician-consensus criticality rate is claimed.

## Automated ensemble vs physician consensus, three-class
- Overall agreement 81.5%; Cohen kappa 0.67 (substantial).
- Per-class kappa: faithful 0.82, drift 0.66, degraded 0.39.
- Class-specific (sensitivity / specificity / PPV / NPV / F1 / support):
  - faithful: 0.91 / 0.91 / 0.93 / 0.88 / 0.92 / 751
  - drift:    0.76 / 0.90 / 0.76 / 0.90 / 0.76 / 380
  - degraded: 0.49 / 0.91 / 0.45 / 0.93 / 0.47 / 164
- Confusion (human rows x auto cols):
  - human faithful -> faithful 685, degraded 47, drift 19
  - human degraded -> faithful 12, degraded 81, drift 71
  - human drift    -> faithful 37, degraded 54, drift 289
  - Dominant error: human-drift and human-degraded items relabeled across the
    degraded/drift boundary; the pipeline more often calls a human-drift item
    degraded than faithful, so it under-detects drift relative to physicians.

## Misclassification-corrected drift (Rogan-Gladen, one pooled confusion matrix, message-clustered bootstrap)
- AUTH: detected 31.9% -> corrected 39.2% (95% CI 27.2-49.6)
- CRIT: detected 33.4% -> corrected 36.2% (95% CI 20.6-50.7)
- CTRL: detected 31.2% -> corrected 32.9% (95% CI 18.4-47.3)
- ALL:  detected 32.0% -> corrected 37.6% (95% CI 25.6-47.8)
- Correction did NOT reduce the detected rate (it rose). The detected rates are
  conservative with respect to physician judgment. Point estimates all lie
  within their CIs; no simplex clipping; no identity-row fallback.
- Assumption (reported): a single corpus-independent confusion matrix, estimated
  on the full 1,295-item validation set, because per-corpus validation subsets
  (CTRL n=70) are too small for stable separate inversions.
